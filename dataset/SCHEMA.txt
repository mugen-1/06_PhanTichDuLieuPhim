QUY UOC DU LIEU NHOM 06 (ca nhom phai theo dung)
- File TSV: cot cach nhau bang dau TAB, UTF-8, KHONG co dong tieu de.
- Trong 1 o khong duoc co dau TAB hay xuong dong (thay bang dau cach).
- Thieu du lieu: de trong. budget/revenue = 0 nghia la thieu.

1) movies (11 cot) - HDFS: /user/nhom06/movies/raw/movies/
   movie_id(INT, TMDB id) | imdb_id(tt...) | title | release_date(YYYY-MM-DD)
   | release_year(INT) | runtime(INT, phut) | genres(ngan bang |, vd Action|Drama)
   | original_language(en, vi...) | budget(BIGINT) | revenue(BIGINT) | overview

2) imdb_ratings (3 cot) - HDFS: /user/nhom06/movies/raw/imdb/
   imdb_id | average_rating(DOUBLE) | num_votes(INT)

3) ratings (4 cot, MovieLens, da doi sang movie_id cua TMDB) - HDFS: /user/nhom06/movies/raw/ratings/
   user_id(INT) | movie_id(INT) | rating(DOUBLE 0.5-5) | ts(epoch giay)

Khoa ghep: imdb_id (TMDB <-> IMDb), movie_id (movies <-> ratings).
Hive: CSDL phim_db cua trungtruc (truong nhom). Sqoop cua trunghieu nap vao CSDL rieng
(vd staging_db) hoac vao dung thu muc HDFS o tren, KHONG ghi de bang trong phim_db.
File lon hon 50 MB: khong day len GitHub, chia se qua Google Drive va ghi link
vao readme.txt. Ban mau nho: dataset/sample/
